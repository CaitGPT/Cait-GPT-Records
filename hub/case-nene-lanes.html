What would you investigate next?

All six player cards finish in columns A and B. Investigating earlier access to the centre could be useful. The final board does not establish which cards were in hand or which placements were possible earlier.

A tactical recommendation needs the earlier board, hand and sequence. This case explains a score; it does not identify a proven losing move or an optimal alternative.
